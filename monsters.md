Here’s a **modernized monster roster** for a **Dungeon Keeper–inspired clone**, built to support **emergent AI**, **room synergies**, and **long-term progression** rather than simple rock-paper-scissors stats. Think of this as a dungeon ecosystem, not a zoo 🕯️👁️

I’ll group them by **function and fantasy**, with notes on *why* each exists.

---

## 🛠 Core Workers & Infrastructure

* **Imp**
  Digs, claims, repairs. Fragile, irreplaceable, surprisingly opinionated.

* **Stone Warden** *(New)*
  Slow, durable worker. Reinforces walls and structures permanently.

---

## ⚔️ Frontline Fighters (Early–Mid)

* **Goblin**
  Cheap, numerous, morale-sensitive pack fighters.

* **Orc**
  Stronger melee bruiser. Demands space and respect.

* **Gnoll** *(New)*
  Fast skirmisher. Excels at patrols and flanking.

* **Kobold** *(New)*
  Trap-savvy fighter. Buffs nearby traps.

---

## 🧠 Casters & Support

* **Warlock**
  Research, ranged magic, spell amplification.

* **Cultist** *(New)*
  Ritual specialist. Generates power through sacrifice synergy.

* **Hexbinder** *(New)*
  Debuffer. Weak damage, strong control.

---

## 🛡 Tanks & Anchors

* **Troll**
  Workshop expert. Slow, hits hard, expensive.

* **Ogre** *(New)*
  Area-control brute. Causes fear waves on impact.

* **Ironbound** *(New)*
  Construct-like creature. Immune to morale, drains mana instead.

---

## ☠️ Stealth, Control & Sabotage

* **Dark Elf**
  Fast, fragile, high DPS, prone to desertion.

* **Shadow Stalker** *(New)*
  Becomes stronger in darkness. Punishes poor lighting.

* **Assassin Wisp** *(New)*
  Ethereal ambusher. Bypasses doors and traps.

---

## 🧟 Undead & Death-Adjacent

* **Skeleton**
  Cheap undead. Disposable, tireless.

* **Vampire**
  Grows stronger through kills. High maintenance.

* **Lich** *(New)*
  Elite caster. Soul-dependent power scaling.

* **Grave Hulk** *(New)*
  Corpse-built juggernaut. Consumes bodies to heal.

---

## 🔥 Demons & High-Risk Power

* **Demon Spawn**
  Strong but volatile. Mood swings violently.

* **Balor** *(New)*
  Endgame demon. Burns mana, breaks walls, ignores fear.

* **Infernal Hound** *(New)*
  Fast hunter. Tracks heroes across rooms.

---

## 🧪 Experimental & Mutated

* **Beastling** *(New)*
  Evolves based on room exposure.

* **Flesh Amalgam** *(New)*
  Grafted creature. Random traits, unstable behavior.

* **Void-Touched** *(New)*
  Corrupted variant. Powerful, attracts attention.

---

## 🧠 Meta & Strategic Creatures

* **Overseer** *(New)*
  Command unit. Buffs nearby creatures’ AI efficiency.

* **Archivist** *(New)*
  Research-focused creature. Converts events into tech progress.

## Design Notes (Why This Roster Works)

* Every monster exists to **stress a system**
* Some monsters fix problems, others create them
* Power is tied to **space, rooms, and behavior**, not raw stats
* Late-game monsters feel *dangerous to own*
