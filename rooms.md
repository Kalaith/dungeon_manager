Here’s a **modernized, forward-looking room list** for a **Dungeon Keeper–inspired clone**, designed to keep the classic fantasy intact while adding **systemic depth, player expression, and long-term replayability**. Think less museum exhibit, more living dungeon that learns bad habits over time 🕯️⚙️

I’ll group these by **design role**, not nostalgia.

---

## 🖤 Core Survival Rooms

*Your dungeon’s pulse.*

* **Dungeon Heart**
  Core structure. Emits influence, generates mana, anchors ownership.

* **Lair**
  Creatures sleep, bond, and develop personal territory traits.

* **Hatchery**
  Food generation. Can mutate output based on dungeon conditions.

* **Treasury**
  Stores gold. Can be raided, cursed, or trapped internally.

---

## ⚙️ Production & Infrastructure

*Where efficiency becomes strategy.*

* **Workshop**
  Crafts traps, doors, siege tools. Output quality scales with layout and specialist creatures.

* **Arcane Archive**
  Research hub. Branching tech trees, forbidden knowledge risks events.

* **Supply Depot** *(New)*
  Buffers materials, reduces travel time, enables logistics optimization.

* **Power Conduit** *(New)*
  Routes mana. Allows localized overclocking of rooms.

---

## 📈 Growth & Specialization

*Creatures become something specific.*

* **Training Hall**
  General combat leveling.

* **Doctrine Chamber** *(New)*
  Choose combat doctrines that reshape creature AI behavior.

* **Combat Pit**
  Risk-reward dueling for rapid XP and traits.

* **Mentor’s Den** *(New)*
  High-level creatures train weaker ones, transferring traits.

---

## 🧠 Control, Morale & Psychology

*Evil is mostly administration.*

* **Prison**
  Holds captives. Conditions affect escape chance and conversion quality.

* **Interrogation Room** *(Modernized Torture)*
  Extract intel, traits, or loyalty at systemic cost.

* **Reeducation Chamber** *(New)*
  Rewrite creature traits. Permanent, dangerous, very effective.

* **Leisure Den** *(Casino successor)*
  Manages morale, addictions, and vice events.

---

## ☠️ Death, Decay & the Occult

*Nothing stays dead unless you’re lazy.*

* **Graveyard**
  Corpses, undead creation, ambient fear generation.

* **Soul Furnace** *(New)*
  Converts deaths into mana, research boosts, or curses.

* **Cryptorium** *(New)*
  Stores souls, enables necromantic upgrades and resurrection limits.

---

## 🛡️ Defense, Flow & Territory

*Make heroes regret geometry.*

* **Guard Post**
  Patrol assignment, zone control.

* **Trap Corridor** *(Roomified traps)*
  Amplifies trap effectiveness when chained.

* **Gatehouse** *(New)*
  Controls dungeon entry flow, taxes intruders with pain.

* **Watch Nexus** *(New)*
  Extends vision, reduces fog-of-war, detects hero intent.

---

## 🔮 Rituals & High-Risk Power

*Where things go wrong gloriously.*

* **Ritual Circle**
  Multi-input spells, sacrifices, world-altering effects.

* **Fate Loom** *(New)*
  Modify incoming events, hero strength, or dungeon destiny.

* **Summoning Vault** *(New)*
  Attract rare creatures or entities with binding conditions.

---

## 🧩 Meta & Strategic Rooms

*Long-term decisions, irreversible mistakes.*

* **Council Chamber** *(New)*
  Choose dungeon-wide policies and laws.

* **Legacy Vault** *(New)*
  Stores persistent unlocks between runs.

* **Obelisk of Memory** *(New)*
  Records deeds. Feeds into reputation and future invasions.

---

---

## Design Principles Behind This List

1. **Rooms create decisions, not chores**
2. **Every room can be dangerous if overused**
3. **Soft failure beats hard punishment**
4. **Psychology is a resource**
5. **Geometry is gameplay**

---

If you want restraint:

* Dungeon Heart
* Lair
* Hatchery
* Treasury
* Workshop
* Training Hall
* Prison
* Guard Post
* Ritual Circle

Everything else unlocks later like forbidden pages in a book that whispers back 📖🕯️
